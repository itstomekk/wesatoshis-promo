# Development

Serve `docs` over HTTP, for example `python -m http.server 8000 --directory docs`, then open http://127.0.0.1:8000/. Three.js and its addons load from jsDelivr, so internet access and WebGL are required.

The 3D model derives from the manufacturer's published enclosure and board CAD; some details are approximated. Third-party assets and brand elements are used with permission, not released under an implied open-source license.
