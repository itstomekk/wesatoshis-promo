    Product information

    The device

    A pocket-sized Bitcoin computer, built to verify, connect and protect without a custodian — your keys stay on the card. Four tactile buttons, a display, a scanner and a battery — everything on the card itself.

    What the card does

    Sourced from wesatoshis.com and its FAQ. Open-hardware card; screen content on this page is a demo.

    Native Bitcoin

    Speaks the Bitcoin protocol natively and broadcasts directly on mainnet over peer-to-peer connections — no Electrum server.

    Lightning (BOLT11)

    Pay BOLT11 invoices using your own Lightning node or your wallet balance. The manufacturer describes a smart contract and another Lightning node.

    Nostr & zaps

    A built-in Nostr client: pair Nostr Wallet Connect to pay invoices and zap friends.

    QR scanning

    A dedicated scanner chip reads invoices, Wi-Fi and node-connection codes on the fly — standard codes; inverted or coloured codes require enabling.

    Your own node

    Connect to a home Bitcoin node via a btcnode: QR code. SPV header verification.

    PIN & decoy mode

    A configurable PIN and decoy mode are described; the manufacturer’s descriptions of their interaction differ.

    SD card

    Stores SPV headers and your encrypted wallet. Firmware updates install offline via SD.

    Open hardware

    Download the enclosure files and print your own case in your preferred materials and colours.

    More capabilities

    Additional features described by the manufacturer (wesatoshis.com FAQ). Not independently verified here.

    Auth-code 2FA

    Use the card as a two-factor device for your AuthCodes.

    DOCPROOF

    Embeds a SHA-256 hash as proof of existence on the blockchain, following the DOCPROOF protocol.

    Block tracking

    Tracks new blocks in real time, including who mined each and per-block stats.

    Mempool & RBF

    Monitors unconfirmed mempool transactions and detects Replace-By-Fee (RBF) transactions.

    Testnet & signet

    Switch the card to Testnet or Signet for testing.

    Sound

    Audible feedback, switchable on or off in settings.

    Secure element

    A secure element is used primarily to generate a new seed phrase during wallet setup.

    Offline updates

    Firmware updates install offline via the SD card.

    Questions, answered

    Summarized manufacturer claims, not independently verified. Official FAQ ↗ · QR generator ↗ · Flashing ↗ · Enclosure ↗

    Purpose and payments

    Is an online card secure?

    The manufacturer argues that insecure code, rather than being online itself, is the risk. This is a claim, not an independent security assessment.

    Is this a replacement for cold storage?

    No. The manufacturer describes it as a decentralized transfer mechanism, not a replacement for cold storage.

    Does it need a central server?

    The manufacturer says the device does not depend on a central server and is designed to work while the Bitcoin network exists.

    What can the card do?

    The manufacturer describes native BTC and Lightning sending and receiving, blockchain data embedding, and other functions.

    Can I pay a Lightning invoice without my own Lightning node?

    The FAQ says a BOLT11 invoice can be paid using either a home Lightning node or the card wallet balance after depositing BTC.

    How are Lightning payments made more private?

    The manufacturer describes locking equivalent BTC in a smart contract and having a random Lightning node pay the invoice, intended to reduce the link between sender and recipient. The claimed privacy is not independently verified.

    How does the card learn that a payment completed?

    The FAQ says the paying Lightning node broadcasts payment status via Nostr, which the card receives in real time.

    Can a Nostr payment confirmation be forged?

    The manufacturer says confirmation relies on invoice preimage and SHA-256 information and claims authenticity. The FAQ wording does not fully explain the verification procedure; do not treat this as an independently verified guarantee.

    Which currencies and payment networks are supported?

    The FAQ lists native BTC and Lightning payments.

    Why a card instead of a mobile app?

    The manufacturer says it wants to avoid dependence on third-party mobile app-store approval.

    Bitcoin, nodes and network

    Can it connect to my Bitcoin node?

    According to the FAQ, a QR code containing node connection information connects it to a home node. The example format is btcnode:127.0.0.1:8333.

    Can a node address use a domain name?

    The FAQ says domains are supported and resolved through DNS.

    Does it require Electrum servers?

    The manufacturer says it uses the native Bitcoin protocol rather than relying on Electrum servers.

    Why might an iPhone hotspot not connect?

    The FAQ suggests enabling Maximize Compatibility in Personal Hotspot settings to use the 2.4 GHz band instead of 5 GHz.

    How many Wi-Fi networks can it remember?

    The FAQ says up to two networks.

    Does it have sound?

    Yes, according to the FAQ; sound can be switched on or off in settings.

    Does it monitor unconfirmed mempool transactions?

    The manufacturer says it does.

    Can it detect Replace-By-Fee transactions?

    The manufacturer says it detects RBF transactions.

    Can it scan inverted or colored QR codes?

    The FAQ says yes after scanning an enabling code. Open the manufacturer’s enabling QR code ↗.

    Can I use Testnet or Signet?

    The manufacturer says both testing networks are supported.

    Is VPN or Tor built in?

    No. The manufacturer says the chip cannot run them directly, but a hotspot device using VPN or Tor can provide the connection. Actual routing depends on the hotspot configuration.

    Can a VPN cause connection problems?

    The FAQ says some VPNs, including ProtonVPN, may interfere with connectivity or transactions. It suggests temporarily disabling the VPN or switching servers; contact support if problems persist. Disabling a VPN changes your privacy protections.

    How do I create a Wi-Fi QR code?

    Use the manufacturer’s QR generator ↗.

    How do I create a btcnode QR code?

    Use the manufacturer’s QR generator ↗ for node connection details.

    Security, privacy and storage

    Can I set a PIN?

    The manufacturer says the card supports a PIN.

    Is there a self-destruction mechanism?

    The manufacturer says there is one, but this FAQ answer does not explain how it works.

    What is the SD card for?

    The manufacturer says it stores blockchain headers from the genesis block onward and encrypted wallet data.

    What if the SD card is lost or empty?

    The FAQ says headers are downloaded and verified again, taking about an hour, and a card of at least 1 GB can be used. It does not explain how encrypted wallet data is recovered; do not assume replacing the card restores your wallet. Ask the manufacturer about backups and recovery.

    Does it use a secure element?

    The FAQ says a secure element is used primarily to generate a new seed phrase during wallet setup.

    Is another person’s Wi-Fi safe to use?

    The manufacturer says sensitive data is not transferred over the network, while noting the Bitcoin protocol is clear text. This is not a guarantee of privacy or safety on untrusted networks.

    What is decoy mode?

    The FAQ says that after a wrong PIN, decoy mode shows a game instead of data. The manufacturer’s homepage describes its interaction with the PIN differently. Ask the manufacturer to clarify before relying on it.

    Tools and hardware

    Can it serve as an AuthCodes 2FA device?

    Yes, according to the manufacturer.

    Can it provide blockchain proof of existence?

    The FAQ says it uses DOCPROOF to embed a SHA-256 hash on the blockchain.

    Can it track new blocks?

    The manufacturer says it tracks new blocks in real time, including the miner and detailed block statistics.

    Can firmware be updated offline?

    The FAQ says updates can be installed offline using the SD card.

    Where do price and fee data come from?

    The manufacturer claims these are fetched in a decentralized way without a central server, but the FAQ does not explain the mechanism.

    Does it have a camera for scanning?

    The FAQ describes a camera with an embedded chip that detects and processes QR codes on the fly.

    What are the battery and charging figures?

    The FAQ advertises a 1000 mAh battery, up to three hours of continuous operation at full power and about 35 minutes to charge. It advises against using it that long in one session.

    Does the card run a Nostr client?

    The FAQ says it has a built-in Nostr client for payment confirmations and decentralized communication.

    What does the fox icon mean?

    According to the FAQ, it indicates incoming Nostr traffic.

    Can I add friends?

    The FAQ says you can add friends by their Nostr public key and zap them.
