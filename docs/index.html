    The card

    A pocket-sized Bitcoin card with its own screen, four buttons and a built-in QR scanner. Native Bitcoin and Lightning, Nostr, swaps and more — pay, receive and manage your sats without a computer or phone app.

    How a payment works

    1. 01

      Scan

      The built-in scanner reads the Lightning invoice in an instant.

    2. 02

      Confirm

      Check the amount on the card's own screen, then approve it.

    3. 03

      Done

      A Lightning node pays the invoice and the confirmation reaches the card in real time over Nostr.

    More on the card

    Native Bitcoin

    Speaks the Bitcoin protocol natively and connects peer-to-peer on mainnet, Testnet or Signet.

    QR scanning

    A dedicated scanner chip reads invoices, Wi-Fi and node codes on the fly.

    PIN & decoy mode

    Lock the card with a PIN. After a wrong PIN, decoy mode shows a game instead of your data.

    SD card

    Stores blockchain headers and your encrypted wallet. Firmware updates install offline from the card.

    Wi-Fi

    Saves up to two networks, one for home and one for on the go. Add them by scanning a Wi-Fi QR code.

    Block tracking

    Follows every new block in real time, including who mined it and detailed block stats.

    Mempool & RBF

    Watches unconfirmed transactions and detects Replace-By-Fee.

    AuthCodes 2FA

    Use the card as a two-factor device for your AuthCodes.

    DOCPROOF

    Embeds a SHA-256 hash on the blockchain as proof of existence.

    Sound

    Audible feedback you can switch on or off in settings.

    Open hardware

    The enclosure is published for download: a 3D-printable back chassis and top cover, a CNC cut path for the screen opening and a STEP board model. Assemble with M2.5 (OD 3.5 mm) heat-set brass inserts, M2.5 × 6 mm stainless screws and a high-transparency polycarbonate sheet.

    General

    Is Wesatoshis meant to replace cold storage?

    No — not at all. Wesatoshis is not a cold storage replacement. It's a transfer mechanism that allows you to move sats without needing a mobile app or computer software.

    What can Wesatoshis do?

    Wesatoshis was designed to handle it all — from sending and receiving native BTC and Lightning sats, to embedding data on the blockchain, and much more.

    What does the fox-shaped icon on the screen mean?

    It indicates incoming NOSTR traffic.

    Why not a mobile app?

    We believe Bitcoin deserves a more secure system—one that doesn't rely on third parties like Apple or Android for store approval. And for the venture capitalists out there, to quote Satoshi Nakamoto - if you don't believe me or don't get it, I don't have time to try to convince you, sorry.

    Features

    Can I pay a Lightning invoice without running my own Lightning node?

    Yes, you can! Simply deposit BTC and the card will automatically give you the option to pay a BOLT11 invoice either using your own Lightning node at home or directly from your wallet balance.

    Can the card function as a 2FA device for my AuthCodes?

    Yes, it can.

    Can the card embed proof of existence in the blockchain?

    Yes, it can. The card follows the DOCPROOF protocol to embed a SHA-256 hash as proof of existence on the blockchain.

    Can the card track real-time blocks?

    Yes, Wesatoshis tracks every new block in real time, including information about who mined it, and displays detailed stats for each block.

    Does Wesatoshis support offline firmware updates?

    Yes, it does — updates can be done offline using the SD card.

    Does Wesatoshis have sound?

    Yes, it does. You can easily turn the sound on or off through the settings.

    Can Wesatoshis scan inverted or colored QR codes?

    Yes, it can. To enable this functionality, please scan the QR Code below.

    Can I switch the card to work on Testnet or Signet for testing?

    Yes, you can.

    Which coins does Wesatoshis support?

    Wesatoshis supports native BTC and Lightning payments, Lightning via Arkade and XMR payments, plus swaps between USDT, USDC, ETH, BTC and XMR via Trocador.

    Does the card support a friends list?

    Yes, you can add friends using their Nostr public key. Once added, you can easily zap them.

    Technical

    Does Wesatoshis rely on a central server to function?

    No — it doesn't. Wesatoshis does not depend on any central server. If you power the device up 5 years from now, it will still work — as long as the Bitcoin network exists.

    Does Wesatoshis use Electrum servers to get Bitcoin data?

    No. Wesatoshis uses the native Bitcoin protocol, just like Bitcoin Core, and does not depend on Electrum servers to function.

    How many WiFi SSID networks can the card support or save for future use?

    The card can save up to 2 WiFi networks — one for your home base and one for use on the go.

    Why does the card come with an SD card?

    The SD card is used to store all the blockchain headers from the genesis block onward and to securely encrypt your wallet data.

    What happens if I lose the SD card or insert an empty one?

    Wesatoshis will automatically download and verify all the blockchain headers—a process that can take about an hour. The device does not depend on the SD card to function, so any SD card with 1GB or more will work perfectly.

    Does Wesatoshis monitor mempool unconfirmed transactions?

    Yes, it does.

    Does Wesatoshis detect Replace-By-Fee (RBF) transactions?

    Yes, it does.

    How does Wesatoshis get Bitcoin price or fee data without relying on a central server?

    Wesatoshis fetches Bitcoin price and fee information with no need for a central server.

    Does Wesatoshis use a camera for scanning?

    Yes, Wesatoshis includes a camera equipped with a special embedded chip that can detect and process QR codes on the fly for fast and secure scanning.

    How long does the battery last?

    At full power, Wesatoshis can run continuously for up to 3 hours, though it's not recommended to use it for that long in one session. A full charge takes about 35 minutes.

    How does the card know when the payment was completed?

    The card receives real-time payment confirmation via NOSTR, an open communication protocol. Once the Lightning node pays the invoice, it broadcasts the status over NOSTR.

    Does Wesatoshis run a NOSTR client?

    Yes, it does. Wesatoshis includes a built-in NOSTR client to receive payment confirmations and communicate securely.

    How-To

    Can Wesatoshis connect to my home Bitcoin node?

    Yes, Wesatoshis can connect to your home Bitcoin node. Just scan a Wi-Fi QR code that includes your node's connection info, and it will automatically connect. The QR code should follow this format: btcnode:127.0.0.1:8333

    Can we use a domain name instead of a direct IP like 127.0.0.1 in btcnode:?

    Yes, Wesatoshis supports domain names. It will automatically resolve the domain using DNS.

    Why does the device not connect to my iPhone's hotspot?

    Your iPhone may be using a 5 GHz band by default. Search for a "Maximize Compatibility" option in the Personal Hotspot settings. By enabling it, your iPhone will switch to the 2.4 GHz band for the hotspot.

    What is "decoy mode" in the settings?

    If you set up a PIN code and someone enters the wrong one, instead of accessing your data, they'll be greeted with a game to play.

    Can using a VPN affect my ability to use the Wesatoshis Card?

    Yes, using a VPN can sometimes interfere with your ability to use the Wesatoshis Card or connect to the network. Certain VPN services, such as ProtonVPN, have been known to cause issues with network connectivity or card transactions. If you're experiencing trouble, we recommend temporarily disabling your VPN or switching to a different server. If you continue to face issues, please reach out to our support team for further assistance.

    Security & Privacy

    How can something like Wesatoshis be secure if it goes online?

    The answer is simple: being online is not a weakness — insecure code is.

    Can I secure my card with a PIN code?

    Yes, you can.

    Does the card have a self-destruction mechanism?

    Yes, it does.

    Does Wesatoshis use a secure element?

    Yes, it does. The secure element is used primarily for generating a new seed phrase during the wallet setup process.

    Is it insecure to use someone else's Wi-Fi?

    No, it's safe. Wesatoshis never transfers any sensitive data over the network (Bitcoin protocol is in clear text).

    How does Wesatoshis ensure private payments?

    The payment system on Wesatoshis works by locking the equivalent BTC amount into a smart contract. The payment is then made by a random Lightning node, breaking the link between sender and recipient, thus enhancing privacy.

    Can the confirmation sent via NOSTR be faked?

    No, it can't. The confirmation is the SHA-256 hash of the invoice's preimage, which only the Lightning node that actually paid the invoice knows. This ensures authenticity.

    Does Wesatoshis come with a built-in VPN? Tor?

    Wesatoshis supports Tor. If your device is connected to a VPN or running Tor and you use it as a hotspot, the card also operates over that secure connection.
