    What the card does

    Sourced from wesatoshis.com and its FAQ. Open-hardware card; screen content on this page is a demo.

    Native Bitcoin

    Speaks the Bitcoin protocol natively and broadcasts directly on mainnet over peer-to-peer connections — no Electrum server.

    Lightning (BOLT11)

    Pay Lightning invoices directly from the card, using your own node or your wallet balance.

    Nostr & zaps

    A built-in Nostr client: pair Nostr Wallet Connect to pay invoices and zap friends.

    QR scanning

    A dedicated scanner chip reads invoices, Wi-Fi and node-connection codes on the fly — standard, inverted or coloured.

    Your own node

    Connect to a home Bitcoin node via a btcnode: QR code. Full SPV verification.

    PIN & decoy mode

    A configurable PIN locks the card; a wrong PIN opens a playable game instead of your data.

    SD card

    Stores SPV headers and your encrypted wallet. Firmware updates install offline via SD.

    Open hardware

    Download the enclosure files and print your own case in your preferred materials and colours.

    More capabilities

    Additional features described by the manufacturer (wesatoshis.com FAQ). Not independently verified here.

    Auth-code 2FA

    Use the card as a two-factor device for your AuthCodes.

    DOCPROOF

    Embeds a SHA-256 hash as proof of existence on the blockchain, following the DOCPROOF protocol.

    Block tracking

    Tracks new blocks in real time, including who mined each and per-block stats.

    Mempool & RBF

    Monitors unconfirmed mempool transactions and detects Replace-By-Fee (RBF) transactions.

    Testnet & signet

    Switch the card to Testnet or Signet for testing.

    Sound

    Audible feedback, switchable on or off in settings.

    Secure element

    A secure element is used primarily to generate a new seed phrase during wallet setup.

    Offline updates

    Firmware updates install offline via the SD card.

    Open hardware

    The enclosure is published for download: a 3D-printable back chassis and top cover, a CNC cut path for the screen opening, and a STEP board model. Assemble with M2.5 (OD 3.5 mm) heat-set brass inserts, M2.5 × 6 mm stainless screws and a high-transparency polycarbonate sheet.

    Notes & sources

    • This page's 3D model, OLED screens and interactions are a demonstration, not a live device or wallet.
    • Per the FAQ, the card is a decentralized transfer mechanism — not a cold-storage replacement.
    • Product, security and certification claims above are manufacturer statements from wesatoshis.com and its FAQ — not independently verified here.
    • SPV verification is not the same as running a full node; the card verifies headers, not the entire chain.
    • The battery in the 3D model is an approximation from a reference photograph. The official site advertises 1000 mAh while the supplied photo shows a 500 mAh cell — the capacity may vary by revision and is not fixed here.
    • The board geometry comes from the published STEP model; enclosure from the published STLs. The supplied "back" PCB photo is not shown because its identity/revision could not be independently verified.
    • CAD geometry is preserved and reproduced with the author's permission.
